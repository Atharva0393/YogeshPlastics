## 2. Shade Netting Product Families & Variant Dynamics

### 2.1 The Two Primary Knitting Constructions

#### Construction A: Tape x Tape (Slit-Film / Flat Ribbon)
- **Yarn Geometry:** Extruded HDPE film slit into flat ribbons (tapes) and knitted on raschel warp-knitting machines.
- **Physical Characteristics:** High surface opacity per gram of polymer; flat geometry provides excellent light diffusion and uniform shade coverage.
- **Mechanical Attributes:** Moderate tensile strength; prone to fluttering stress if unsupported over wide spans.
- **Typical Density Range:** 35%, 50%, 75%, 90% shade factors.
- **Typical Weight Range:** 80 to 140 GSM.
- **Best Suited For:** Horticultural seedling nurseries, open-field vegetable cultivation, home garden shade, perimeter privacy screens, temporary construction fencing.

#### Construction B: Mono x Mono (Extruded Round Monofilament)
- **Yarn Geometry:** Extruded single-strand round filaments (similar to heavy fishing line) in both warp and weft directions.
- **Physical Characteristics:** Round yarn creates an open, highly porous structural matrix that allows laminar wind passage with minimal aerodynamic drag.
- **Mechanical Attributes:** Superior burst strength, exceptional tear resistance, high dimensional stability under mechanical cable tension.
- **Typical Density Range:** 35%, 50% shade factors.
- **Typical Weight Range:** 100 to 135 GSM.
- **Best Suited For:** Permanent net houses, permanent greenhouse roofs, coastal or high-wind agricultural regions, hail-prone zones.

#### Construction C: Tape x Mono Hybrid (Combination)
- **Yarn Geometry:** Monofilament in the warp (lengthwise load-bearing) and flat tape in the weft (crosswise shading).
- **Physical Characteristics:** Combines the high structural tensile strength of monofilament with the superior shading coverage of tape ribbons.
- **Common Density Range:** 50%, 75%, 85% shade factors.

## 3. Shade Percentage Matrix & Agricultural Applications

| Shade Factor | Solar Attenuation | Light Transmitted | Documented Agricultural Suitability | Typical Commercial Applications |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **35%** | 35% blocked | ~65% transmitted | Tomato, pepper, leafy greens in moderate summer heat; enhances photosynthesis while buffering peak solar midday scorch. | Open-field row crop shading, nursery acclimatization |
| **50%** | 50% blocked | ~50% transmitted | General plant nursery propagation, general vegetable farming, shaded polyhouse covers, seedling beds. | Plant nurseries, horticulture, polyhouse roof covers |
| **75%** | 75% blocked | ~25% transmitted | Delicate floriculture, shade-loving foliage (ferns, anthuriums, orchids), severe summer heat protection. | Orchid houses, floriculture polyhouses, cattle shed cooling |
| **85% – 90%** | 85–90% blocked | 10–15% transmitted | Not recommended for most photosynthesizing agricultural crops; reserved for livestock heat shelters and human comfort. | Vehicle parking, construction scaffolding, privacy fencing |

## 4. Key Terminology Glossary for Buyers & Engineers

- **HDPE (High-Density Polyethylene):** A thermoplastic polymer known for high strength-to-density ratio, chemical inertia, and moisture resistance, making it the global standard for outdoor netting.
- **GSM (Grams per Square Metre):** The metric measuring the mass of polymer per unit area ($g/m^2$). Higher GSM generally indicates greater yarn mass, thicker knit, and superior longevity.
- **UV Stabilization (HALS):** The chemical integration of Hindered Amine Light Stabilizers into the virgin polymer melt before extrusion. HALS scavenges free radicals generated by solar UV radiation, preventing polymer chain degradation and brittleness.
- **Lock-Stitch Knitting:** A specialized raschel knitting method where individual stitches interlock. If a strand is punctured or severed, the knit will not ladder or run across the roll.
- **Selvedge:** The reinforced, tightly knitted outer edges along the lengthwise boundaries of the roll, providing anchor points for cable ties, eyelets, or stitching.
- **Aluminet (Thermal Screen):** Knitted screen incorporating metallized aluminum coating that reflects thermal infrared radiation, operating as an optical mirror rather than an absorbing barrier.
