## 1. Problem-Driven Architecture Overview

In agricultural materials procurement, buyers do not simply purchase "netting"; they invest in **climate management, crop survival, and operational asset protection**. Matching the customer to the right product requires analyzing four interdependent environmental variables:

1. **Biological Solar Tolerance:** What crop species or asset is being protected?
2. **Ambient Solar & Wind Exposure:** Is the structure located in an arid high-radiation plain, an open coastal wind corridor, or an urban setting?
3. **Mounting Infrastructure:** Is the installation on a rigid steel polyhouse, a flexible wooden nursery frame, or a cable-tensioned tensile sail?
4. **Seasonal vs. Multi-Year Permanence:** Is the net expected to remain in place through monsoon storms and winter, or is it a seasonal summer deployment?

## 2. Sector-by-Sector Application Dossiers

---

### Application Sector 1: Plant Nurseries & Seedling Propagation Beds

- **Customer Objective:**
  Protect fragile emergent seedlings and saplings from solar leaf scorching, retain ambient relative humidity around germinating beds, and moderate microclimate temperature during midday heat spikes.
- **Environmental Context:**
  Moderate wind, continuous high humidity from misting/irrigation, delicate young foliage vulnerable to extreme radiation.
- **Recommended Shading Factor:**
  **50% Shade Density** (Agro Green Shade Net 50%).
- **Preferred Construction:**
  Tape x Tape or permeable lock-stitch HDPE mesh. Allows uniform diffuse illumination while preventing heat traps.
- **Practical Installation Notes:**
  Install at least 2.0 to 2.5 metres above seedling trays to allow natural convective airflow beneath the netting. Ensure support purlins are rounded to prevent abrasion.
- **Questions to Ask Customer:**
  1. What types of seedlings are being cultivated (vegetable, timber, ornamental)?
  2. What are the dimensions of the nursery bays (length and width)?
  3. Is there overhead misting or drip irrigation in place?

---

### Application Sector 2: Commercial Polyhouse & Greenhouse Roofing

- **Customer Objective:**
  Moderate internal greenhouse temperatures during peak summer months without blocking essential photosynthetically active radiation (PAR).
- **Environmental Context:**
  High continuous thermal radiation, exposure to wind lift on roof ridges, interaction with underlying greenhouse polythene films.
- **Recommended Shading Factor:**
  **50% to 75% Shade Density** (Heavy Agro Shade Net 75% or 50% depending on regional climate).
- **Preferred Construction:**
  Mono x Mono or dense Tape x Mono hybrid. Superior tear resistance against roof frame friction and high wind uplift.
- **Practical Installation Notes:**
  Can be deployed externally over the polyfilm arch (external shading) or installed as an internal retractable thermal curtain using guide wires and manual/motorized roll-up units.
- **Questions to Ask Customer:**
  1. What is the peak summer daytime temperature inside the greenhouse?
  2. Is external roof shading or internal curtain shading planned?
  3. What are the roof arch dimensions and gutter-to-gutter spans?

---

### Application Sector 3: Floriculture & Heat-Sensitive Crops (Orchids, Anthuriums)

- **Customer Objective:**
  Eliminate direct solar beams that bleach petals and burn delicate leaf tips, while maintaining high humidity and diffuse light levels.
- **Environmental Context:**
  High-value commercial flowers require precise photoperiod and solar regulation; excess heat directly causes flower drop and reduced stem length.
- **Recommended Shading Factor:**
  **75% Shade Density** (Heavy Agro Shade Net 75%).
- **Preferred Construction:**
  Dense lock-stitch mesh in Dark Green or White/Neutral.
- **Practical Installation Notes:**
  Provide adequate perimeter ventilation; 75% nets can trap hot air if sidewalls are completely sealed.
- **Questions to Ask Customer:**
  1. What flower varieties are cultivated (e.g., Anthurium, Dendrobium, Carnation)?
  2. Are side curtains also required for crosswind buffering?

---

### Application Sector 4: Open-Field Crop Protection & Orchard Canopies

- **Customer Objective:**
  Shield field-grown vegetables (tomatoes, capsicum, melons) and fruit trees from sunburn, sudden localized hailstorms, and large bird predation.
- **Environmental Context:**
  High open wind speeds, wide unbraced structural spans, seasonal weather extremes.
- **Recommended Shading Factor:**
  **35% to 50% Shade Density** (50% Agro Green Shade Net).
- **Preferred Construction:**
  Monofilament or reinforced lock-stitch raschel mesh with heavy reinforced selvedges.
- **Practical Installation Notes:**
  Requires high-tensile wire infrastructure with anchor earth screws. Selvedges must be secured with durable clips to distribute mechanical load.
- **Questions to Ask Customer:**
  1. What is the total acreage or field perimeter to be covered?
  2. What is the prevailing wind direction and typical peak wind speed?

---

### Application Sector 5: Livestock Housing & Dairy Barn Cooling

- **Customer Objective:**
  Reduce heat stress in dairy cattle, sheep, and poultry. Heat stress in dairy herds directly reduces daily milk yield by 15% to 30% and suppresses feed intake.
- **Environmental Context:**
  High ammonia levels, open cross-ventilated sheds, severe solar exposure on tin or asbestos roofs.
- **Recommended Shading Factor:**
  **75% to 90% Shade Density** (Commercial Shade Cloth 90% or Heavy Agro 75%).
- **Preferred Construction:**
  Heavy-duty UV-stabilized mesh in Dark Green or Black.
- **Practical Installation Notes:**
  Mount 0.5m above corrugated metal roofs as an over-roof solar shield to block radiant roof heating, or deploy along open shed facades to block low-angle morning/evening sun.
- **Questions to Ask Customer:**
  1. Is the net to be placed above a tin roof or as a side-curtain sun buffer?
  2. What is the length and height of the open barn faces?

---

### Application Sector 6: Commercial, Civil & Architectural Installations

- **Customer Objective:**
  Provide high-density solar shade and visual privacy for vehicle parking lots, commercial storage yards, school playgrounds, sports facilities, and building construction perimeters.
- **Environmental Context:**
  High mechanical tension, aesthetic expectations, human occupancy comfort, dust and debris containment.
- **Recommended Shading Factor:**
  **85% to 90% Shade Density** (Commercial Shade Cloth 90%, Architectural Tensile Netting 85%).
- **Preferred Construction:**
  Tension-grade lock-stitch knit formulated for dimensional stability under cable tension.
- **Practical Installation Notes:**
  Corners must feature reinforced webbing and stainless steel D-rings to withstand cable winching. Tensioning must account for seasonal temperature expansion and contraction.
- **Questions to Ask Customer:**
  1. What is the support structure (steel posts, cable perimeter, scaffolding)?
  2. Are custom cut-to-size finished panels with edge hems needed, or continuous commercial rolls?

## 3. Inquiry Intake Checklist for Sales & Technical Consultation

When a customer contacts Yogesh Plastics, gathering the following parameters ensures an accurate, technically sound recommendation:

```
[ ] 1. Primary Intended Use (Agriculture, Nursery, Polyhouse, Dairy, Car Park, Privacy)
[ ] 2. Specific Crop / Asset to Protect
[ ] 3. Target Area Dimensions (Length × Width or Total Square Metres)
[ ] 4. Preferred Shade Factor (35%, 50%, 75%, 85%, 90% or "Needs Guidance")
[ ] 5. Installation Frame (Steel Frame, Cable/Wire, Timber Poles, Over-Roof)
[ ] 6. Geographic Location / Environmental Severity (Wind, High Heat, Arid)
[ ] 7. Preferred Supply Format (Continuous Commercial Rolls vs. Custom Fabricated Dimensions)
```
